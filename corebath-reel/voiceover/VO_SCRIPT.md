## Recording brief for a voice artist

- **Voice:** Indian-English, warm and confident, mid-30s. Calm, like Apple's narration, never shouty.
- **Pace:** relaxed but tight. Each line must finish inside its window above.
- **Pain lines (03–07):** slightly flat and tired, like you've been through it.
- **"Not anymore." (08):** a pause before it, then a warm, assured turn. This is the emotional pivot.
- **Solution lines (09–15):** brighter, with a smile in the voice.
- **Pronunciation:**
  - **BYOB:** say the letters, "B-Y-O-B".
  - **Corebath:** stress the first syllable, *CORE*-bath.
- **Delivery:** record each line as a separate take with 1 s of silence on either side. Deliver as WAV, 48 kHz, 24-bit, no music, no effects.
- Each line drops into the timing slot above, and the existing sound effects automatically dip under the voice.
