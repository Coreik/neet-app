# Corebath BYOB: 90-day launch and growth strategy

**Business:** bathroom renovation, Bengaluru apartments
**Offer:** pick a bathroom you love → we build that look → fixed price from ₹1.49 lakh → handed over in 21 days → 5-year waterproofing warranty
**Primary conversion:** WhatsApp conversation → site visit → signed order

> Numbers marked *(assumption)* are planning estimates from public Indian benchmarks. Replace them with your real data after the first 14 days.

---

## 1. Goal and unit economics (start here)

### 1.1 Set the target
| Item | Planning value | Your number |
|---|---|---|
| Average order value | ₹1.8 lakh *(assumption: ₹1.49L entry + upgrades)* | |
| Gross margin | 25% → ₹45,000 per bathroom *(assumption)* | |
| Bathrooms you can deliver per month (crew capacity) | 15 *(assumption)* | |
| **Maximum affordable acquisition cost (CAC)** | ≤ ₹12,000 per signed bathroom (about 27% of margin) | |

### 1.2 Work the funnel backwards
| Stage | Conversion *(assumption)* | Per month for 15 bathrooms |
|---|---|---|
| Signed orders | — | **15** |
| Quotes given | 40% of quotes → signed | 38 |
| Site visits | 80% of visits → quote | 47 |
| Qualified leads | 35% of leads → visit | 134 |
| Ad spend at ₹400 cost per lead | Indian interiors CPL is typically ₹250–600 | **≈ ₹54,000/month** |
| CAC | ₹54,000 ÷ 15 | **≈ ₹3,600 per bathroom** ✅ well under ₹12,000 |

**Takeaway:** paid ads are very affordable for a ₹1.5L+ ticket. The real constraints are **lead handling and delivery capacity**, not ad budget.

## 2. Positioning and message

**One idea:** *Saw a bathroom you love? We build that exact look in your home: fixed price, 21 days.*

| Pillar | Proof you must show |
|---|---|
| **Your look** (BYOB) | The bath board; pick from inspiration |
| **Fixed price** | Price from ₹1.49L; written inclusions and exclusions |
| **21 days** | Delay guarantee (optional); real timelines |
| **No leaks** | 5-year waterproofing warranty; process footage |
| **Top brands** | Jaquar, Cera, Kohler, Grohe, Kajaria (only ones you actually supply) |

**Voice:** Apple-clean visuals with Zomato-witty copy. Short, human, very Bengaluru.
**Own the BYOB joke:** "Bengaluru's favourite BYOB just got a lot cleaner." / "BYOB. Not that one. The one with tiles."

### Audiences (personas)
| Persona | Who | Trigger | Best message |
|---|---|---|---|
| **A · Upgrader** (core) | 28–42, IT/professional, owns a 5–15-year-old apartment | Dated bathroom, leaks, wants a "Pinterest" bathroom | Desire: "Build the one you saved" |
| **B · Fixer** | 35–55, owner with an active leak or seepage complaint | Neighbour or RWA complaint, damp walls | Pain: leaks, warranty, 21 days |
| **C · Parents' safety** | 30–45 buying for parents (55+) | Fall risk, anti-skid, grab bars | Safety, fixed price, fast |
| **D · New handover** | Just got keys to a new flat | Builder-grade bathroom | Upgrade before moving in |

## 3. Foundations (Day −21 to −15). Do not launch without these.

**Brand and legal**
- [ ] One brand name everywhere (Corebath *or* Corespace)
- [ ] Offer terms in writing: fixed-price inclusions and exclusions, warranty card, 21-day clock start (design sign-off + material confirmation), delay guarantee cap
- [ ] Image rights: use your own or licensed images in ads; credit inspiration images in organic posts

**Instagram and Facebook**
- [ ] Professional account, bio, highlights (BYOB · Before/After · Process · FAQ · Reviews)
- [ ] Meta Business Portfolio (business.facebook.com) with Instagram, the Facebook Page, the ad account and a payment method
- [ ] Verify the business; set the ad account currency to INR and timezone to Asia/Kolkata

**WhatsApp (your conversion engine)**
- [ ] WhatsApp Business app (or API via a provider) on a dedicated number
- [ ] Greeting message, away message, quick replies, and a **catalogue** with 3 packages
- [ ] Comment automation (ManyChat or Meta's built-in automations): "BYOB" comment → DM with the WhatsApp link and price list

**Tracking**
- [ ] Lead tracker (Google Sheet or a free CRM): Date · Name · Phone · Area · Persona · Source ad · Status (New → Qualified → Visit booked → Visited → Quoted → Won/Lost) · Value · Lost reason
- [ ] Unique "first message" text per ad (e.g. "Hi, I saw BYOB-LEAK") so you know which ad drove each chat
- [ ] Google Business Profile with photos, services and service areas; ask every past client for a review

**Team**
- [ ] One named owner of WhatsApp leads, replying within **5 minutes** from 9 am–9 pm
- [ ] Site-visit slots blocked in a calendar; quote turnaround **48 hours**

**Content capture (ongoing)**
- [ ] Film every current site: before, during waterproofing, after, crew faces, a 15-second customer quote at handover

**Stories (daily):** this-or-that polls (Set 2 `B`), personality quiz slides (Set 2 `C`), behind-the-scenes from sites, contest entries, countdown (Set 1 `06`).

### 5.2 The contest: "Bengaluru's Worst Bathroom"
- **Mechanic:** post your worst bathroom (leak, broken tiles, mould) as a reel or photo, tag @corebath, use #ByobBengaluru. Must be a homeowner in Bengaluru.
- **Prize:** one free waterproofing job or bathroom refresh (set a ₹ cap). Two runners-up get a free leak inspection.
- **Why it matters:** user content + shares + your first public "transformation story". Film the winner's makeover; it becomes your best ad in month two.
- **Rules:** a dated T&C post, the judging date, and "not sponsored, administered or endorsed by Instagram".

### 5.3 Paid seeding (this is the paid distribution behind the warm-up)
**Purpose:** a new account's organic reach is close to zero. Spend a little to put the best posts in front of the right Bengaluru homeowners, and to build **warm audiences** to retarget at launch.

**Campaign 1: "P1 · Seed · Video views"**
- Ads Manager → **Create** → Objective **Awareness**
- Performance goal: **maximise ThruPlay** (people who watch 15 s or the full video)
- Audience: Location **Bengaluru**, with pins on your target areas (Whitefield, Sarjapur Road, HSR, Koramangala, Electronic City, Indiranagar, JP Nagar, Jayanagar, Marathahalli, BTM, Banashankari, Malleshwaram), radius 3–5 km each; Age 28–55; Advantage+ audience **on**, with suggestions "home improvement, interior design, apartment"
- Placements: Advantage+ (Reels and Stories will take most of it)
- Budget: **₹300/day**
- Ads: the before/after reel, the leak carousel (as a video or carousel), founder video

**Campaign 2: "P1 · Seed · Engagement"**
- Objective **Engagement** → goal **maximise engagement**
- Same audience, **₹200/day**
- Ads: the contest post, the meme cards, bath board (use **existing posts** so likes and comments stack on the organic post)

**Build these custom audiences now** (Audiences → Create → Custom audience):
1. **Video viewers 50%**, last 30 days
2. **Instagram engagers**, last 60 days
3. **Profile visitors**, last 30 days
4. **WhatsApp/Messenger contacts** (as they come in)

**Phase 1 spend:** ₹500 × 14 days ≈ **₹7,000**.

## 6. Phase 2: launch (Day 0 to 14)

### 6.1 Launch day checklist
- [ ] Post the launch film (`corebath-byob-reel-vo.mp4`) with the final caption; pin it with the leak carousel and the before/after reel
- [ ] Announce contest results (or the extension)
- [ ] Turn off the Phase 1 engagement campaign; keep video seeding at ₹300/day as a feeder
- [ ] Turn on the campaigns below

### 6.2 Campaign structure

**Campaign A: "P2 · Prospecting · WhatsApp" (about 70% of spend)**
- Objective: **Leads** → conversion location **Messaging apps → WhatsApp** (if your account shows it). Otherwise **Engagement → Messaging apps → WhatsApp**, goal *maximise conversations*.
- Budget: **Advantage campaign budget ₹1,400/day**
- Ad set A1, "Bengaluru core": same geo pins; age 28–55; Advantage+ audience on
- Ad set A2, "Parents' safety": same geo; age 30–50; message angle C (safety)
- Ads (3–4 per ad set):
  1. Launch film, 17 s cut (desire + pain)
  2. Leak-first hook variant ("Is your bathroom leaking again?")
  3. Offer-first static or carousel ("New bathroom in 21 days. Fixed price from ₹1.49L.")
  4. Before/after real project
- WhatsApp **pre-filled message** per ad: "Hi! I want to BYOB (ref: A1-LEAK)"
- **Instant reply** in the WhatsApp greeting: thanks, the price list and 4 qualifying questions (see section 8)

**Campaign B: "P2 · Retargeting · WhatsApp" (about 20%)**
- Same objective; **₹400/day**
- Audience: video viewers 50% (30 d) + IG engagers (60 d) + profile visitors (30 d); **exclude** people who already messaged (WhatsApp contacts)
- Ads: proof and offer, i.e. before/after, waterproofing process, "5-year warranty", "Late? We pay ₹1,000/day" (only if adopted), a testimonial once available

**Campaign C: "P2 · Test · Instant form" (about 10%)**
- Objective **Leads** → **Instant forms** → form type **Higher intent** (adds a review step and cuts junk)
- Questions: Name, Phone, Area (multiple choice), "Do you own the flat?" (Yes/No), "How many bathrooms?", "When do you want to start?" (This month / 1–3 months / Later)
- **₹200/day**. Compares form leads against WhatsApp leads on *cost per site visit*.

**Phase 2 spend:** ≈ ₹2,000–2,300/day × 14 ≈ **₹30,000**.

### 6.3 The first 72 hours: don't touch it
Meta's delivery needs about 50 results or 3–4 days to settle. Don't edit ads in the first 72 hours unless something is broken (a wrong link, a rejected ad).

## 7. Phase 3: optimise (Day 15 to 30)

### 7.1 Decision rules (rules of thumb; adjust to your data)
| Signal | Threshold | Action |
|---|---|---|
| 3-second hook rate (3 s views ÷ impressions) | < 25% after 3,000 impressions | Replace the opening 2 seconds |
| Link or click-to-WhatsApp rate | < 0.8% | New angle or offer |
| Cost per WhatsApp conversation | > 2× the account average after ₹3,000 spend | Pause the ad |
| Cost per site visit | Best ad set | Move budget to it, **+20% every 3 days** |
| Ad frequency (prospecting) | > 3 in 7 days | Refresh the creative |

### 7.2 Weekly review (every Monday, 30 min)
1. Spend → conversations → qualified → visits → quotes → won, **per ad**
2. Top 2 ads: scale. Bottom 2: pause. Add 2 new variations of the winner (new hook, same body)
3. Read 10 WhatsApp chats: what objections keep coming up? Answer them in next week's creatives
4. Update the lost-reasons tally (price, timing, trust, renter, out of area)

### 7.3 Creative refresh cadence
- **Every week:** 2 new hooks on the winning ad (first 2 seconds only)
- **Every 2 weeks:** 1 new real-project reel
- **Monthly:** a new angle (safety, new handover, monsoon, festive)

## 8. Lead handling: where campaigns are actually won or lost

### 8.1 Speed
- First reply in **under 5 minutes**. After an hour, contact rates drop sharply.

### 8.2 First reply (WhatsApp template)
> Hi {name}! 👋 Thanks for your interest in BYOB by Corebath.
> Here's our price list 📄 (fixed price from ₹1.49 lakh).
> To plan your bathroom, could you share:
> 1️⃣ Your area in Bengaluru
> 2️⃣ Do you own the flat? (Yes/No)
> 3️⃣ How many bathrooms?
> 4️⃣ When would you like to start?
> And if you have a bathroom you love, send the photo! 🛁

### 8.3 Qualify
| Qualified (book a visit) | Nurture | Disqualify politely |
|---|---|---|
| Owner, in service area, starting within 3 months | Owner, starting in 3–6 months | Renter, out of area |

### 8.4 Book the visit in the same chat
> "Our designer can visit on **Thursday 11 am** or **Saturday 4 pm**. Which works better?" (Always offer two options, never "when are you free?")

### 8.5 Follow-up cadence
- **Day 0:** reply + qualify + offer a visit
- **Day 1:** "Did you get a chance to look at the price list?" + a before/after photo
- **Day 3:** a short reel (waterproofing process) + "Shall I hold Saturday's slot?"
- **Day 7:** a testimonial or "only 3 visit slots left this week"
- **Day 14:** a final soft check-in, then move to monthly nurture

### 8.6 Objection answers
| Objection | Answer |
|---|---|
| "Too expensive" | Show what's included (demolition → waterproofing → tiles → fittings); compare to "quoted ₹1.5L, paid ₹1.72L" |
| "Can you really do 21 days?" | Show the timeline + the delay guarantee (if adopted) + a past project's dates |
| "What if it leaks?" | 5-year waterproofing warranty, with the card in writing |
| "I'll think about it" | Offer a free site visit and quote with no obligation |

### 8.7 After handover: referrals
- Ask for a Google review + a 15-second video at handover
- Referral reward: ₹___ credit or a gift for every referred signed order *(set an amount you can afford)*

## 9. Phase 4: scale (Day 31 to 90)

### 9.1 Meta
- **Lookalike audiences** (once you have 100+): seed with "qualified leads" and later "won customers" (upload phone numbers), lookalike 1–3% India filtered to Bengaluru
- **Advantage+ campaigns:** test against the manual structure once you have 30+ conversions a week
- **Partnership ads:** run creator content through the creator's handle (more trust, often cheaper)
- Budget: ₹3,000–4,000/day if capacity allows *(only scale as fast as you can deliver)*

### 9.2 Google Search (high-intent demand)
- Campaign "Search · Bathroom renovation Bengaluru", **₹700–1,000/day**
- Keyword themes (phrase or exact match):
  - bathroom renovation bangalore / bengaluru
  - bathroom remodel / makeover bangalore
  - bathroom waterproofing bangalore
  - bathroom leakage repair bangalore
- Negatives: jobs, course, DIY, cheap, salary, PDF, design software
- Ad copy: "Bathroom Renovation in 21 Days | Fixed Price from ₹1.49L | 5-Yr Waterproofing Warranty"
- Landing: a one-page site or a WhatsApp click-to-chat (a call extension + location from the Google Business Profile)

### 9.3 Creators and partners
- **2–3 Bengaluru home, apartment or lifestyle micro-creators** (10k–100k followers). Brief: the "my bathroom before Corebath" story, or a review of a real handover. Pricing varies widely, so ask for quotes and pay part in cash and part in services if possible.
- **Collab posts** (both accounts post it) + **partnership ads** to boost them

### 9.4 Local and community channels
- **MyGate / NoBroker / apartment apps:** these platforms offer ads inside gated communities. Ask for their Bengaluru rates and target your areas.
- **RWA / apartment association tie-ups:** a free "leak check day" in one large society, with a notice-board poster and a QR code to WhatsApp
- **NoBroker / Reddit (r/bangalore) / Quora:** helpful answers about leaks and renovation costs (no spam; link only when relevant)
- **Hardware and tile showrooms:** a referral arrangement with local dealers for customers who need installation

## 12. Risks and compliance

| Risk | Mitigation |
|---|---|
| Ad rejection (images or claims) | Own or licensed images; no "#1" or "best" without proof; avoid before/after framing that implies a guaranteed result for all |
| ASCI complaint (misleading claims) | Every claim (price, warranty, 21 days) backed in writing; exclusions in the caption |
| More leads than you can serve | Cap the budget to delivery capacity; show "limited slots per month" honestly |
| Slow replies kill the ROI | A named owner, an SLA, automation for the first reply |
| Quality issues hurt reviews | Supervisor checklist; waterproofing ponding test documented on video |
| Brand confusion (Corebath vs Corespace) | Resolve before Day −14 |
| Contest legal issues | A clear T&C post, a prize cap, a judging date, no purchase required |
